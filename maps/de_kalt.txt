The secret location of an antiterrorism communications bunker has been compromised. Several terrorist factions would like to sieze the opportunity to sabotage the bunker, thereby scarring the image of the international antiterrorism effort.

Terrorists:
Destroy the communications bunker (target A) or the historical statue (target B).

Counter-Terrorists:
Prevent the terrorists from destroying either target.
