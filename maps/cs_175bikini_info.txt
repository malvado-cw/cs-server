Installing
----------
Unzip into the \Cstrike folder with "Use Folder/Path Names" on.

There are two female hostage models and sounds that replace the default hostage.mdl,
scientist.mdl and HOSx.WAV files, installing these files is optional. You may want
to backup the original model and sound files first before overwriting them.

PODbot
------
The PODbot v2.x .PWF waypoint file goes in \Cstrike\Podbot\Wptdefault

Story
-----
175 Bikini Drive is the Bikini Hangout HQ where its employees work and play.
It is also used as a photoshoot location for their super models (wish i could
work there :-) ), during one the photoshoots it is overrun by terrorists
who take the super models hostage.


Information
-----------
Hostage rescue map based on a mansion scenario for a Bikini Hangout mapping
contest - http://www.bikinihangout.com
in conjunction with CS Central - http://www.cscentral.com

AFAIK a female CT+T skin pack was supposed to be produced by CSC for the
competition, it'll be on the CSC or Bikini Hangout sites.


Notes
-----
There are some areas on tops of walls that have r_speeds that are higher than
I would like, these areas could've been blocked off but have been left in to
give players more options. 

PODbots nav most of the map ok, the area around the gazebo(?) thingy was a
compromise - couldn't put a camp waypoint inside it as bots that got onto the
roof tried to get to it by going thru' the roof and got stuck :(
